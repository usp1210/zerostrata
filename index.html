Model: multivariate hypergeometric — the D events are treated as a simple random sample of the N subjects, ignoring treatment effect, differential stratum hazards, and censoring patterns. Under a real treatment effect the two arms are not exchangeable, so read these as design-stage approximations. P(0) for a single stratum is C(N−n, D)⁄C(N, D); the "at least one empty" probability uses inclusion–exclusion over subsets of the checked strata — stratum counts are dependent, so it is not 1 − Π(1 − P₀).
